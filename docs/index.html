THE MISSIONHow a match plays

Every team owns a bunker - and carries the keys to it. Your job is to take theirs while keeping yours.

01

Steal the keys

Every enemy soldier carries a key to their own bunker. Take them down and grab what they drop - stolen keys are shared, so any teammate can open the door.

02

Crack the rooms

Each key opens a puzzle room with a random terminal: code locks, safe dials, signal tuners, pressure valves, timing locks, steady-hand and pump challenges - while your squad holds the door.

03

Rob the vault

Solve every room and the vault opens. Grab gold, jewels and the golden idol.

04

Bank the loot

Carry it back to your camp or vehicle to score. Owners who hold their bunker for a minute lock it again.

WHAT'S INSIDEFeatures

An old-school raycaster engine pushed into a full FPS: real heights, rooftops, vehicles, weather and chaos.

4 game modes - incl. WAR

Heist, Capture the Flag, Team Deathmatch - and WAR SIMULATOR: 16 vs 16, three capture points, planes and big vehicle fleets.

Weather & time of day

Clear, cloudy, rain, thunderstorms, fog, snow and dust storms - from morning to pitch-black night with headlights, lamps and muzzle flashes.

7 puzzle terminals

Quick hands, not memory: timing locks, steady-hand tracking, pressure pumps, safe dials and more - every bunker rolls a fresh mix.

5 combat vehicles

Tank, Broneviks armoured car, motorcycle with sidecar MG, jeep and rocket truck. Driver and gunner seats, real 3D models, turrets that follow your aim.

Base mortars

Man your camp's mortar and shell the battlefield from a top-down tactical view - a quarter of the map in range.

Random events

Map-wide air strikes, supply drops, storms, radio jamming, gold rush and bounties - several every match.

Full 3D movement

Look up and down, climb stairs and watchtowers, fight from rooftops, shoot through windows, crouch behind sandbags. Headshots count.

8 battlefields

Valley, desert, snow, ruined town, red canyon, harbor, marshlands and coastline - with four different base layouts. Or roll a random map.

Play your way

Solo with AI squads, split-screen co-op, or LAN with automatic server discovery (Hamachi works too). Teammate name tags and health bars keep your squad together.

Space / CJump / crouch
RReload
123Primary, sidearm, knife (or mouse wheel)
GGrenade, smoke or medkit
EUse: vehicles, key doors, puzzle terminals, mortar
FSwitch vehicle seat
Tab / EscScoreboard / pause & options

LAN & Hamachi

One player clicks HOST LAN GAME; everyone else clicks JOIN LAN GAME - hosts on your network show up automatically, including over Hamachi. The host picks map, mode, 2 or 4 teams, weather and time of day; empty slots are filled with AI. Set your name and team in the lobby; the first player of each pair picks the vehicle.

  • Allow the game through your firewall when asked (UDP ports 7777 & 7778).
  • On Hamachi, make sure the network is allowed on "Public" networks in Windows Firewall.
  • Can't see the host? Use Direct connect with the host's IP shown in their lobby.

PATCH NOTESChangelog

v1.32026-11-27LATEST
  • New mode: WAR SIMULATOR - always 2 teams of 16 (AI fills empty slots). Capture and hold points A, B and C: every held point scores, holding all three for 60 s wins outright. Respawn at the safest point your team holds.
  • Planes: fighter-bomber, heavy bomber and dive bomber with a pilot seat (W/S throttle, the mouse is the control stick: steer + climb/dive) and a bomber seat with a bombsight. Take off from the airfield next to your camp, don't stall, don't bail out. AI aircrews fly and bomb too.

  • War sky at dusk and night: rockets arc over the battlefield, bursts light up the clouds, distant booms.
  • Vehicles: you are now either the driver OR the gunner - never both, even alone (switch seats with F). Drivers no longer fire the hull MG.
  • Fixed: the objectives panel was missing in Capture the Flag and Team Deathmatch.
  • LAN games support up to 32 players; network traffic for big matches cut by about 3x.
v1.22026-11-27
